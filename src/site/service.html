General Medicine & Patient Care

Listen Carefully. Diagnose Clearly. Care Completely. Our Services

Every visit begins with listening. A clear diagnosis, a plan built around your life, and follow-up that keeps you healthy — at the clinic, at home or in an emergency.
General Consultation
Unhurried visits for any health concern — we listen first, then examine and explain.
Every visit starts with listening. Dr. Anthony takes time to understand your symptoms, history and concerns, performs a careful examination and explains the diagnosis and next steps clearly — so you leave with answers you can trust.
Read More
Full Health Check-up
A complete check of blood pressure, blood sugar, cholesterol, weight and BMI.
You don't have to wait until you're sick to see your doctor. A regular check-up tracks your vitals — blood pressure, blood sugar, cholesterol, weight and BMI — and catches small changes early, when they are easiest to treat.
Read More
At-Home Visits
Medical care in the comfort of your home for elderly, bedridden or unwell patients.
When getting to the clinic is difficult, the doctor comes to you. At-home visits bring examination, treatment and follow-up to elderly relatives, bedridden patients and anyone too unwell to travel across Keserwan.
Read More
Emergency Care
Prompt medical attention for sudden illness, injuries and urgent symptoms.
Sudden fever, breathing difficulty, chest discomfort or an injury needs fast, calm assessment. Dr. Anthony provides prompt emergency evaluation and first treatment, and arranges hospital transfer whenever it is needed.
Read More
Chronic Disease Care
Ongoing management of high blood pressure, diabetes, cholesterol and thyroid conditions.
Long-term conditions such as high blood pressure, diabetes, high cholesterol and thyroid disorders need steady follow-up. Regular reviews, clear targets and practical lifestyle advice help you stay in control and avoid complications.
Read More
Lab & Imaging Review
Clear explanations of your blood tests, X-rays and medical imaging results.
Results can be confusing. Bring your blood tests, X-rays, ultrasounds or scans and Dr. Anthony will walk you through what they mean, what is normal and what — if anything — needs to happen next.
Read More
Medication Review
A safe review of all your medicines, doses and possible interactions.
Taking several medicines? A medication review checks every prescription and supplement for the right dose, possible side effects and interactions — simplifying your treatment and keeping it safe.
Read More
Flu & Vaccination
Seasonal flu, colds and infections — diagnosis, treatment and adult vaccination.
Winter brings colds, flu and chest infections. Get the right diagnosis and treatment early, learn the facts behind common myths, and protect yourself and your family with the seasonal flu vaccine.
Read More
Women's Health & Screening
Preventive check-ups and early detection, including breast health guidance.
Early detection saves lives. Dr. Anthony offers preventive check-ups for women, breast health guidance and screening referrals — with clear advice all year round, not only in October.
Read More
Lifestyle & Prevention
Practical guidance on nutrition, weight, activity, sleep and quitting smoking.
Better health often starts with small daily choices. Personalised advice on nutrition, weight, physical activity, sleep, stress and quitting smoking helps prevent disease and improves how you feel every day.
Read More
Wound Care & First Aid
Cleaning, dressing and follow-up of minor wounds, burns and injuries.
Minor cuts, burns, sprains and injuries are cleaned, treated and dressed with care, with follow-up visits to make sure everything heals well and without infection.
Read More
Specialist Referral
Coordinated referrals to trusted specialists through a wide referral network.
When you need a specialist, you don't have to find your way alone. Dr. Anthony's wide referral network connects you with the right specialist, shares your history and follows up on the results with you.
Read More
Physical Examination
Thorough physical exams for school, work, sports and travel.
A complete head-to-toe physical examination for school, work, sports clubs or travel — including vital signs, heart and lung examination and a medical certificate when required.

Family Health
Caring for every generation — children, parents and grandparents.
As a general practitioner, Dr. Anthony cares for the whole family. Knowing your family's history makes diagnosis faster, prevention smarter and follow-up easier for children, adults and seniors alike.

About Dr. Anthony El Kai

Dr. Anthony El Kai, MD is a graduate of the University of Balamand. During his clinical training at Saint George University Medical Center in Beirut he worked across the emergency department, cardiac and intensive care, internal medicine, ENT, ophthalmology, radiology and anesthesia.

Today he leads his own general practice in Rayfoun, Keserwan, with more than 10,000 consultations and a wide referral network. His approach is simple: good healthcare starts with a doctor who takes the time to listen — and better health often begins with small, lasting lifestyle changes.
