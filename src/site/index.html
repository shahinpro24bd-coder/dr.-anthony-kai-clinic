About Dr. Anthony El Kai

Who is Dr. Anthony El Kai?

Dr. Anthony El Kai, MD, is a graduate of the University of Balamand. During his clinical training at Saint George University Medical Center in Beirut, he worked across the emergency department, cardiac and intensive care, internal medicine, ENT, ophthalmology, radiology and anesthesia.

Today he leads his own general practice in Rayfoun, Keserwan, with more than 10,000 consultations and a wide referral network. His approach is simple: good healthcare starts with a doctor who takes the time to listen — and better health often begins with small, lasting lifestyle changes.
